# Cutting SOP — Version 3.0

## Purpose
Ensure consistent fabric cutting quality, minimize waste, and maintain dimensional accuracy across all production batches.

## Equipment
- Automatic fabric spreader (Eastman 627X)
- CNC cutting machine (Gerber Z1)
- Manual cutting tables (backup only)
- Weight scales (±5 g accuracy)

## Pre-Cutting Checklist
1. Verify fabric roll matches PO shade band
2. Check fabric width against spec (min 140 cm usable)
3. Record roll weight and length
4. Lay fabric for 4 hours minimum to relax tension
5. Check spread tension: 2–3% stretch max

## Spreading Parameters
| Layer Count | Max Height | Max Plies | Tolerance |
|-------------|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Light (<200 GSM) | 15 cm | 150 | ±2 mm |
| Medium (200–300 GSM) | 12 cm | 80 | ±2 mm |
| Heavy (>300 GSM) | 10 cm | 50 | ±3 mm |

## Cutting Parameters
- Blade speed: Auto (CNC) / 2 strokes/sec (manual)
- Vacuum pressure: ≥0.6 bar
- Marker efficiency target: ≥85%

## Quality Checks
- First ply check: measure 3 random pieces against marker
- Edge alignment: ±2 mm from marker line
- Fraying: maximum 3 mm on cut edges
- Shade check: compare top vs. bottom ply under D65 light

## Defect Handling
- **Fraying >3 mm**: Re-cut single ply, log fabric defect
- **Misalignment >2 mm**: Stop machine, recalibrate vacuum
- **Shade variance**: Quarantine roll, notify QC supervisor
