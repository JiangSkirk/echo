# Sewing & Assembly SOP — Version 2.0

## Purpose
Standardize sewing operations to achieve consistent stitch quality, seam strength, and dimensional stability.

## Equipment
- Juki DDL-8700 (straight stitch, main seams)
- Juki MO-6716S (overlock, edge finishing)
- Brother PR1050X (embroidery, labels)
- Steam iron with vacuum table

## Thread Specifications
| Seam Type | Thread | Stitch Density | Needle |
|-----------|--------|----------------|--------|
| Main seams | T-40 spun polyester | 10–12 SPI | DB×1 #14 |
| Overlock | T-30 spun polyester | 12–14 SPI | DC×1 #16 |
| Topstitch | T-40 cotton-wrapped | 8–10 SPI | DB×1 #14 |
| Bartack | T-60 bonded nylon | 42 stitches | T×1 #18 |

## Assembly Sequence
1. **Shoulder seams** — Plain stitch, press open
2. **Neck rib** — Attach with overlock, topstitch 6 mm from edge
3. **Sleeves** — Set-in sleeve, plain stitch, press toward body
4. **Side seams** — Plain stitch + overlock, press open
5. **Hem** — Double-needle coverstitch, 12 mm from edge
6. **Label** — Tack at center back neck, 4 bartacks

## Sewing Tolerances
- Seam allowance: ±1 mm from spec
- Stitch density: ±1 SPI
- Skew: Maximum 1% of garment width
- Shrinkage after pressing: ≤2%

## In-Process Inspection
- **First article**: Full measurement check against spec
- **Every 50 pcs**: Spot check 2 pcs for stitch density
- **Hourly**: Needle change log (every 8 hours or 2000 pcs)

## Rework Criteria
- **Skip stitch ≤2 per 10 cm**: Acceptable, no action
- **Skip stitch >2 per 10 cm**: Rework seam, change needle
- **Broken needle**: Stop line, metal detector sweep, log incident
- **Oil stain**: Spot clean; if permanent, downgrade to B-grade
